Why We Ask for Analytics Access

Liminal is designed to help creators grow smarter — not just track views and likes, but understand why content performs the way it does.

What This Access Lets Us Do:

  • Analyze your audience retention (how long people watch your videos)
  • Identify drop-off points and engagement patterns
  • Feed that data into our AI to give you personalized growth suggestions

What This Access Does Not Let Us Do:

  • We cannot post, delete, or edit your videos
  • We cannot change your channel settings
  • We do not share or sell your analytics data

All access is granted through Google's official OAuth system, which is strictly monitored and read-only in this case.
